Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 9, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Kerados ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website and services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Waitlist Information

When you join our waitlist, we collect:

  • First Name - To personalize communications
  • Last Name - To personalize communications
  • Email Address - To send waitlist updates and product announcements
  • Reason for Interest - To understand user needs and improve our product
  • Consent Records - To document your permission for data processing
  • Timestamp - To track when you joined the waitlist

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

We automatically collect:

  • IP Address - For security and analytics
  • Browser Type - To ensure compatibility
  • Device Information - To optimize user experience
  • Cookies - See Cookie Policy section below

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for:

  • Waitlist Management - To notify you when Kerados becomes available
  • Product Updates - To send announcements about new features (with consent)
  • Customer Support - To respond to your inquiries
  • Security - To prevent fraud and protect our services
  • Legal Compliance - To comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Product Improvement - To analyze user needs and improve Kerados

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process your personal data based on:

  • Consent - You have given explicit permission for waitlist signup
  • Legitimate Interests - To operate our business and improve our services
  • Legal Obligations - To comply with applicable laws

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

5.1 Service Providers

  • Supabase - Database hosting (All waitlist data, United States)
  • Render.com - Web hosting (IP addresses, logs, United States)

Data Processing Agreements: We maintain written Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) with all service providers who process your personal data.

5.2 We Do NOT:

  • ❌ Sell your personal information to third parties
  • ❌ Share your data for marketing purposes without consent
  • ❌ Use your data for profiling or automated decision-making

6. Data Retention

  • Waitlist Data: Until product launch + 30 days, or until you request deletion
  • Logs and Analytics: 90 days
  • Consent Records: 3 years (legal requirement)

7. Your Rights

7.1 GDPR Rights (EEA Users)

You have the right to:

  • Access - Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification - Correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure - Request deletion ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction - Limit how we process your data
  • Portability - Receive your data in machine-readable format
  • Object - Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw Consent - Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge Complaint - File complaint with your data protection authority

7.2 CCPA/CPRA Rights (California Residents)

You have the right to:

  • Know - Know what personal information we collect
  • Access - Request a copy (up to twice per year)
  • Delete - Request deletion of your data
  • Correct - Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Opt-Out - Opt out of sale or sharing
  • Non-Discrimination - Not be discriminated against for exercising rights

Exercise Your Rights: Email privacy@kerados.com. We respond within 45 days (CCPA) or 30 days (GDPR).

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • ✅ Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • ✅ Encryption at rest (database-level)
  • ✅ Rate limiting and DDoS protection
  • ✅ Input validation and sanitization
  • ✅ Regular security audits
  • ✅ Access controls and authentication

9. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a breach, we will:

  • Notify affected users within 72 hours (GDPR) or without unreasonable delay (CCPA)
  • Report to relevant authorities as required by law
  • Provide information about the breach and mitigation steps

10. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies for:

  • Essential Cookies - Required for site functionality
  • Preference Cookies - To remember your settings (requires consent)

Control cookies through our cookie banner or browser settings.

11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to the United States. We ensure protection through:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
  • Data Processing Agreements with service providers
  • Adherence to GDPR principles

12. Children's Privacy

Kerados is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

13. Contact Information

For privacy-related questions:

  • Email: privacy@kerados.com
  • CCPA Requests: California Privacy Protection Agency at cppa.ca.gov
